# Language Pack: English (en)

Use these conventions when the internal summary is produced in English.

## Confidentiality notice (mandatory, verbatim)

The document must begin with exactly this block:

```markdown
# INTERNAL USE ONLY

This document contains internal planning information, delivery assumptions, effort estimates, resource
allocation considerations, commercial inputs, risks, dependencies, and project planning data.

It is intended exclusively for internal use and must not be shared with customers, suppliers,
subcontractors, partners, or any external party without explicit authorization.

The information contained herein is intended to support commercial evaluation, proposal preparation,
delivery planning, staffing decisions, and project governance activities.
```

## Effort-only statement (verbatim)

> Cost calculations have not been included because no approved internal costing model was provided or
> authorized.

## Unapproved cost figures (verbatim, Commercial Clarifications item)

> The source quotes cost figures that no approved costing model backs; they are not reproduced here.
> Confirm the costing basis before pricing.

## Conventions

- Effort unit label: `PD` (Person-Days) or `MD` (Man-Days) — match the source's convention.
- Date format: `YYYY-MM-DD` or `D Month YYYY`.
- Prefer action-oriented phrasing in Recommended Next Actions ("Confirm…", "Validate…", "Staff…").
